# ecommerce-business-analytics
E-commerce sales and customer analytics project using Excel.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales and customer data to identify business trends, customer segments, regional performance, and operational outcomes.

The goal was to use data analysis to turn raw business data into actionable insights that could support better business decisions.

## Business Questions

- Which regions generate the most sales?
- Which regions have the highest profit margins?
- Which customer segments generate the most revenue?
- How do loyal, returning, and new customers compare?
- What are the major order outcomes?
- What business areas may require further investigation?

## Tools & Skills

- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables
- PivotCharts
- Data Cleaning
- Data Analysis
- Business Insights
- Data Visualization

## Key Findings

- **South** was the largest sales region with **$55.59M** in net sales.
- **North** had the highest profit margin at **46.10%**.
- **Consumer customers** generated **$97.20M** in net sales.
- **Loyal customers** generated **$166.18M** in net sales.
- **Completed orders** accounted for **$144.20M** in net sales.
- Overall net sales were approximately **$177.13M** with an overall profit margin of **42.99%**.

## Business Recommendations

- Investigate the products and customers driving strong performance in the South region.
- Analyze the product mix, pricing, and customer mix contributing to North's higher profit margin.
- Study consumer purchasing behavior to understand the largest customer segment.
- Investigate customer retention factors among loyal customers.
- Examine cancellations and returns to identify operational improvement opportunities.

## Project Outcome

The analysis demonstrates how Excel-based business analytics can be used to identify performance differences, understand customer behavior, and generate actionable business insights from raw e-commerce data.
